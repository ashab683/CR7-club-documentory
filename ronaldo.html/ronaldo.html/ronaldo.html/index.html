Cristiano Ronaldo - Football Journey

From Sporting lisban to Al Nassr

Sporting Lisbon (2002-2003)

Cristiano Ronaldo began his professional career at Sporting CP in Portugal, where his talent caught the eyes of Europe’s biggest clubs.Joining the club’s youth academy at just 12 years old, he impressed coaches with his speed, skill, and relentless work ethic. Ronaldo made his first-team debut for Sporting Lisbon in 2002 at the age of 17, showcasing flashes of brilliance that hinted at his future greatness. His standout performance in a friendly against Manchester United in 2003 convinced Sir Alex Ferguson to sign him, marking the start of Ronaldo’s rise to global stardom

Manchester United (2003-2009, 2021-2022)

Cristiano Ronaldo’s journey at Manchester United began in 2003 when he signed as a teenager from Sporting Lisbon. Under Sir Alex Ferguson’s guidance, he transformed from a talented winger into one of the world’s best players. During his first spell at United, Ronaldo dazzled fans with his incredible dribbling, powerful shots, and trademark free-kicks. He won three Premier League titles, an FA Cup, two League Cups, and the UEFA Champions League in 2008, the same year he claimed his first Ballon d’Or. After six unforgettable years, he left for Real Madrid in 2009 as a club legend and one of the best players in Premier League history.

Real Madrid (2009-2018)

At Real Madrid, Ronaldo became the club’s all-time top scorer. He won 4 Champions League titles, 2 La Liga titles, 3 FIFA Club World Cups, 4 Ballon d’Or awards (2013, 2014, 2016, 2017) and several Golden Boots.Cristiano Ronaldo’s journey at Real Madrid is one of football’s greatest chapters. Joining the club in 2009 for a then-world record fee, he quickly became their talisman and all-time top scorer. At Madrid, Ronaldo evolved into a goal machine, delivering astonishing performances season after season. He won four Champions League titles, two La Liga titles, and multiple domestic trophies, while breaking countless records and earning four more Ballon d’Or awards. His fierce determination, incredible goals, and iconic moments at the Bernabéu cemented his status as a Real Madrid legend and one of the greatest players to ever play the game.

Juventus (2018-2021)

Ronaldo won 2 Serie A titles, 1 Coppa Italia and continued breaking records as Juventus’ top scorer during his time in Italy.Cristiano Ronaldo joined Juventus in 2018, bringing his winning mentality and superstar presence to the Italian giants. At Juventus, he continued to shine, becoming the team’s top scorer season after season. Ronaldo helped the club win two Serie A titles and a Coppa Italia, consistently delivering crucial goals and big performances. Despite high expectations in the Champions League, European glory eluded him at Juve, but his impact on Italian football was undeniable. Ronaldo’s time in Turin showed his relentless drive to succeed in different leagues and solidified his legacy as one of football’s greatest ever.

Al Nassr (2023-present)

Ronaldo joined Saudi Arabian club Al Nassr, bringing global attention to the Saudi Pro League while adding goals and breaking new records. marking a new chapter in his legendary career as he brought his star power to Saudi Arabian football. Joining the Riyadh-based club, Ronaldo became one of the biggest names ever to play in the Saudi Pro League, attracting global attention and inspiring a wave of interest in football across the region. At Al Nassr, he continued to score goals and deliver standout performances, demonstrating his enduring passion and professionalism even in a new league.
